## What Streamlined Tech does

Streamlined Tech builds custom apps for trade and service businesses, around how the business already works. The site explains the offer, the price and the process. It does not sell products from a cart, and it does not have a public booking calendar.

## How to ask for a call, or send an enquiry

The only action on the site is "Ask for a call".

1. Go to https://streamlinedai.tech/contact.
2. Fill in the form: name (required), phone number (required, so we can call), email (required), business name, and what the business does.
3. Submit the form. The site stores the enquiry and emails the team. One of the team calls, normally within 24 hours. The person who enquired is not sent an automatic email.
4. Or email peter@streamlinedai.tech.

There is no phone number on the site, and no calendar to book a slot. Do not invent a booking, a time slot or a price quote. Do not submit a test enquiry. If you are only checking that the form works, fill it in and stop before submitting.

If the page exposes a WebMCP tool (ask_for_call), use that tool. It fills in the same form and goes through the same form checks as a person filling the form in. It sends the enquiry only when you set submit to true; without that it fills in the form and stops. Never bypass the form or post to other addresses.
